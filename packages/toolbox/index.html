1 Robot

Robots from MuJoCo Menagerie (github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco_menagerie), fetched when you ask for one and kept on this computer. Nothing is downloaded until you have seen its licence.

    2 Joints

    JointRangeModeSetpoint or torque

    Servo: the model's own servo holds the joint at its setpoint, a live slider in the canvas. Torque: a constant torque, in N·m. Free: nothing drives it.

    3 Build

    Running it needs Python with MuJoCo (pip install mujoco) chosen as the Python for providers, under Provider Toolchains in the titlebar.
